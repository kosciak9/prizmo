# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Prizmo is for serious Pokémon TCG players who want a more capable practice and analysis environment than the official client provides. Primary users include competitive players, serious learners, coaches, testers, and spectators following high-level play.

Users may be playing a normal game, replaying a previous game, testing lines against themselves, reviewing cup games, checking draw percentages, or constructing exact board-state puzzles for practice.

## Product Purpose

Prizmo is a serious Pokémon TCG play and analysis platform. It supports regular play like PTCGL, then goes deeper: replay, self-play, tournament follow mode, draw probability inspection, playstate puzzles, and coaching-oriented review.

Success means the interface helps players understand the game state, test better lines, and build sharper competitive intuition without burying the card table under generic analytics.

Prizmo is intended to stay focused on Pokémon TCG rather than broaden into a generic TCG platform.

## Brand Personality

Competitive instrument: precise, composed, trustworthy.

Prizmo should feel premium and serious under competitive pressure. It should preserve the feeling of playing cards while making game structure, replay history, odds, and decision points inspectable when they matter.

## Anti-references

Prizmo should not feel like PTCGL's casual glossy game client, a mobile game reward loop, or a spreadsheet-style simulator.

Avoid childish game polish, casino-like effects, generic SaaS dashboard patterns, and dry tables that erase the feeling of card play.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the table primary. Analysis tools should support play, not replace the board.
2. Make hidden structure inspectable. Logs, odds, prompts, and replay steps should clarify decisions without breaking the game flow.
3. Earn trust through precision. State, legality, timing, and player perspective must be unambiguous.
4. Support serious practice modes. Replay, self-play, puzzles, and tournament follow views should feel like first-class workflows.
5. Stay composed under pressure. The interface should be readable, calm, and fast during complex turns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline. Preserve visible focus states, keyboard access for core actions, reduced-motion support, sufficient contrast, and non-color-only communication for game state, legality, warnings, and player prompts.
