## Key Hostile Mechanics

### Dragapult

Dragapult ex pressures Alakazam on two axes at once: large Active damage and bench damage counters from Phantom Dive. Alakazam MEG #56's Powerful Hand places 2 damage counters per card in hand, so it needs 16 cards in hand to place 320 counters onto Dragapult ex. That requirement is difficult under pressure.

Stock Nighttime Mine builds may not be enough into pure Dragapult. Battle Cage deserves aggressive testing, but only if logs prove it meaningfully denies Dragapult's multi-target pressure.

### Rocket's Articuno

Rocket's decks are worse for Alakazam than the archetype names alone imply because representative Rocket's Honchkrow and Rocket's Mewtwo lists include Team Rocket's Articuno. Team Rocket's Articuno DRI #51 has Repelling Veil, preventing effects of attacks done to Basic Team Rocket's Pokémon. This can blank Alakazam's damage-counter placement against protected Basic Team Rocket's targets.

The user notes that Alakazam can barely win into Rocket's decks using Articuno. Future testing should preserve exact board states where Articuno changes the prize map.

### Festival Lead / Dipplin

Dipplin TWM #18 can attack twice with Festival Grounds. That makes the prize trade extremely hostile: Festival can often take two single-prize KOs in one turn while Alakazam takes only one prize back.

Handheld Fan can sometimes deny one Festival Lead attack by moving Energy, but it is a tech patch rather than a solved matchup. Future logs should distinguish between "Fan bought a full turn" and "Fan only delayed the unfavorable trade."
