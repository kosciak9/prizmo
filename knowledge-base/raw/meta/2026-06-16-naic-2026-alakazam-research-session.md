Conclusion from the research session: Alakazam Dudunsparce was popular at NAIC 2026 but underperformed competitively. It has strong rogue-counter potential, especially into Slowking, Beedrill, Raging Bolt Ogerpon, and Ogerpon / Meganium / Hydrapple, but the real top field was hostile. Dragapult is the largest data-backed problem; Rocket's Articuno and Festival Lead / Dipplin are structurally hostile matchups that should not be treated as ordinary variance.
